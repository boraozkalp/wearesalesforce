Guide

We treat sales force management as one end-to-end system in 11 modules. Each module explains the management logic first, then shows how to apply it in the field with checklists, examples and templates.

01

The role and objectives of the sales force

A sales team is not a group that drives around visiting customers; it is the commercial system that executes the company’s customer strategy in the field. This module defines vision, mission and the two measures of success.

What is the sales force for?
02

Customer selection and portfolio management

Portfolios instead of territories, analysis instead of lists. The system that decides which customer is assigned to whom, how often they are seen and under which management model.

Which customers do we focus on?
03

Target setting and KPI management

“Sell more” is not a target. How quantitative and qualitative targets are built, how a company target is broken down to customer level, and the SMART logic.

What will grow, and with whom?
04

Performance monitoring and action management

You cannot manage a team you do not measure. The performance cycle that tracks results and behaviour together, variance analysis, and a matrix for reading rep performance.

How will results and behaviour be measured?
05

New customer acquisition and canvassing

New customers don’t arrive by chance. A measurable acquisition process from defining the target customer to the second order.

How do we win new customers systematically?
06

The customer visit and the sales conversation

An eight-step visit structure from preparation to analysis; questioning, listening, benefit-led persuasion, objection handling, closing and negotiation.

What will the visit change for the customer?
07

Sales force organisation

Who is responsible for what, and who decides what? Roles, span of control, interfaces with other departments and organisation models for each stage of a company’s development.

Who is responsible for what?
08

Team profiles, job descriptions and competency levels

Is the right person in the right role? Three hiring criteria, role-based profiles, sample job descriptions, four competency levels and a 90-day model.

Is the right person in the right role?
09

Pay, bonus and incentive systems

Which behaviour are we rewarding? The fixed-variable balance, features of a good bonus scheme, KPI choice, threshold and cap, and bonus design by role.

Which behaviour are we rewarding?
10

Training, coaching and competency development

Training is a system, not an event. Onboarding, basic and advanced training, measuring impact; the field coaching day, feedback rules and coaching models.

How will behaviour be developed?
11

Compliance, ethics and field conduct

What are our limits on the way to the target? Practical field rules for competition law, anti-corruption, personal data, conflicts of interest and expense integrity.
