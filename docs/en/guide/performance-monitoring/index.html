Performance monitoring and action management

You cannot manage a team you do not measure. The performance cycle that tracks results and behaviour together, variance analysis, and a matrix for reading rep performance.

Performance reading matrix

Why measure?

1. Return on investment

A sales force is expensive. The link between team cost and the extra profit it generates must be visible.

2. Market and wallet share

Is sales growth coming from market growth, or from share won with customers?

3. Competency and behaviour

Is the visit quality that produces results improving? Today’s behaviour is tomorrow’s result.

Quantitative and qualitative performance

Quantitative performance is tracked in numbers: sales, gross profit, active customers, frequency, penetration, new customers, collections, cost of sales as a share of sales.

Qualitative performance is tracked through observation: visit preparation, questioning, persuasion, objection handling, closing, reporting discipline, customer feedback. Its main tool is the manager’s coaching observation in the field.

The performance cycle

  • Daily: are visit plans and notes in the system? Are promises in the calendar?
  • Weekly: status vs target, missed visits, customers at risk. The manager has at least one coaching contact.
  • Monthly: a one-to-one with every rep: KPIs, causes of variance and an action plan.
  • Quarterly: portfolio and segment review, target revision, training needs.
  • Annual: competency appraisal, development plan, career and succession conversation.

Variance analysis: why did we miss?

The first reflex should not be “the rep isn’t working”. Look for the cause in three places:

Source Typical causes Whose action
Customer Business shrinking, seasonality, change of owner, closure Portfolio and target revision
Company Stock-outs, delivery problems, price competitiveness, promotion errors Relevant departments, manager
Rep Visit frequency, preparation, product knowledge, closing skills Coaching and training

Writing an action plan

A good action plan answers four questions: What? Who? By when? How will it be measured? Pick no more than three actions; ten-point plans don’t get executed.

Action Owner Date Measure
Trial the breakfast category with 8 B-segment customers Rep Month end 5 new category purchases
Meet operations about 3 customers with late deliveries Manager 1 week Complaints closed

Reading rep performance: the matrix

High results · low behaviour → Fragile success

Good results, weak method; may be one big customer or luck. Coach on visit quality.

High results · high behaviour → Star

Sustain and stretch: delegate, set challenging goals, offer a mentoring role.

Low results · low behaviour → Intensive support

A clear 60–90 day development plan, frequent coaching, explicit expectations.

Low results · high behaviour → On the right track

Check portfolio potential and target realism; support patiently.

Recommended dashboard

A one-page weekly dashboard should include: sales and gross profit vs target · active and lost customers · visit compliance by segment · top 10 opportunities and top 10 risks · overdue receivables · this week’s coaching note.

Manager check questions

  • Do I hold a one-to-one performance meeting with every rep at least monthly?
  • When there is a variance, do I separate customer, company and rep causes?
  • Do I assess qualitative performance with a standard form or by impression?
  • Do I check whether last month’s action plans were carried out?
